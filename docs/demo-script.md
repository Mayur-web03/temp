# Demo script (4 minutes)

1. **Command picture.** ALPHA-12 is red. Snow is on the ridge. Days of stock ~3.5 on Extreme Cold Clothing.
2. **Read the KPIs.** Stockout high. Road delivery confidence low. Four posts in the critical set.
3. **Open the map.** Ground tracks are warm/cool by confidence. Dashed lines are air.
4. **Forecast.** P10/P50/P90 band. Drivers: altitude, snow, high mission activity.
5. **Simulator.** Snow closure is already injected. Toggle **Route damaged** — road dies. Toggle it off.
6. **Commit recommended plan.** Helicopter + cross-level from BRAVO-7. Stockout falls. Confidence rises.
7. **Generate staff note.** That is the executable output for G3/G4.

Hero question, out loud:

> Will the supply reach before stockout? If not, what should we do?
